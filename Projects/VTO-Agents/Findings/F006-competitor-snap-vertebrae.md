# F006 — Snap AR / Vertebrae Competitor Profile

**Project:** [[VTO]] · Source task: [[T006 Competitor-Landscape-Map]]

## One-line takeaway
Snap acquired Vertebrae (2021/2022) for its AR commerce platform — now powers AR try-on for brands within Snapchat and web AR. Significant for social commerce AR but NOT competing on Shopify for eyewear specifically.

## Context
- **Snap Inc.** (NYSE: SNAP) — parent company of Snapchat
- **Vertebrae** — AR commerce platform, acquired by Snap to power AR Shopping
- **Product:** Snap AR Enterprise Services (ARES) — AR try-on for brands on Snapchat + web
- **Focus:** Social AR commerce, AR advertising, brand experiences on Snapchat

## Live Demo Quality Assessment

No dedicated eyewear demo accessible. Snap AR try-on is experienced through:
- Snapchat Lens carousel (branded lenses)
- Web AR embeds on brand sites

**NOTE:** Scores reflect Snapchat's AR quality generally; eyewear-specific quality varies by brand implementation.

## Tech Approach
- **Type:** True 3D AR (face mesh, world tracking, hand tracking)
- **Platform:** Snapchat (iOS/Android), Camera Kit (web/iOS/Android SDK), Lens Studio (creation)
- **Technology:** Proprietary face mesh, hand tracking, body tracking, world mesh
- **AR Shopping:** Try-on lenses for eyewear, makeup, clothing, shoes, accessories
- **Scale:** 250M+ daily Snapchat users engaging with AR

## Shopify Support
- **App store:** NO — not listed on Shopify App Store
- **Integration:** Snap AR Shopping Lenses + brand partnerships; Camera Kit SDK for custom apps
- **Target:** Enterprise brands, not Shopify SMB merchants

## Churn Signals
- Public company (SNAP) — financial stability
- AR is core to Snap's strategy — not a side business
- Vertebrae acquisition shows commitment to AR commerce
- Potential churn risk: Snap's focus may shift away from commerce AR if ad revenue pressures increase

## Pricing (context only)
- Enterprise/contact sales
- Lens creation through Lens Studio (free) + AR Shopping distribution (paid)
- Likely expensive for SMBs

## Sources
- Snap AR: https://ar.snap.com/ (referenced, not directly visited)
- Snap-Vertebrae acquisition: industry knowledge (Crunchbase/TechCrunch, 2021/2022)
- Snapchat AR: public knowledge of Snap's AR platform

## Verdict
Snap AR is the most-used AR platform globally but not a direct competitor on Shopify. Their eyewear try-on exists as branded Snapchat lenses — a different use case (social engagement vs purchase decision). Relevant mostly as a quality benchmark: our try-on should be at least as good as a Snapchat lens.
