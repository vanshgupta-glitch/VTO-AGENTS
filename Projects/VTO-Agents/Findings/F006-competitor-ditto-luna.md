# F006 — Luna / Ditto Competitor Profile (ACQUIRED — NOT ACTIVE COMPETITOR)

**Project:** [[VTO]] · Source task: [[T006 Competitor-Landscape-Map]]

## One-line takeaway
Ditto (rebranded to Luna, domain luna.io) was an independent eyewear VTO pioneer — but was acquired by 1-800 Contacts (~2022), then by **FittingBox** in 2023, and its ecommerce platform was shut down. It is NO LONGER an independent competitor; its technology was absorbed into FittingBox.

## History
- **Founded:** 2011, Mountain View, CA (Kate Endress, Sergey Surkov, Dmitry Kornilov)
- **Original model:** Eyewear ecommerce retailer with virtual fitting using facial landmark detection (pupils, ears, cheekbones)
- **Key IP:** Ditto held patents in virtual eyewear fitting; 1-800 Contacts bought a patent to "squelch competition" (EFF, 2013)
- **Rebrand:** Changed to Luna at some point, domain luna.io
- **Acquisition 1:** Acquired by 1-800 Contacts (~2022); ecommerce site at ditto.com was closed (Milled notification)
- **Acquisition 2:** FittingBox acquired Ditto Technologies Inc. (2023) — technology absorbed into FittingBox's VTO platform

## Tech Approach (historical)
- 3D virtual fitting using facial landmark detection
- Measured pupils, ears, cheekbones and other facial features
- Produced images of glasses on customer's face
- Patent portfolio now owned by FittingBox

## Current Status
- **NOT an active independent competitor**
- All technology, patents, and presumably customer relationships absorbed by FittingBox
- The ditto.com domain now points to an unrelated database company
- luna.io appears to redirect/not serve eyewear content

## Implications for VTO Project
- Ditto/Luna's technology is part of FittingBox now — assess via [[F001-fittingbox-summary]]
- Their patent portfolio (now FittingBox's) was already mapped in [[T002 Patent-IP-Mapping]]
- No need to separately benchmark — FittingBox is the active competitor carrying their DNA
