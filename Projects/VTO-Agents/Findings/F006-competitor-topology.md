# F006 — Topology Eyewear Competitor Profile

**Project:** [[VTO]] · Source task: [[T006 Competitor-Landscape-Map]]

## One-line takeaway
Topology Eyewear is the quality leader for precision eyewear fitting — not just try-on but true "Digital Opticianry" with millimeter-accurate frame and lens measurements for advanced prescriptions. Premium B2B, no Shopify presence, contact-sales pricing.

## Tech Approach
- **Type:** 3D face scanning + precision measurement (not just AR overlay)
- **Platform:** Appears to be mobile app-based (iOS/Android) with web integration
- **Face measurement:** Takes precise frame AND lens measurements — pupillary distance, segment heights, frame fitting parameters
- **Differentiation:** "Digital Opticianry" — built for optometrists, not just consumers
- **"The most realistic virtual try-on"** — emphasis on photorealism over AR speed
- **Measurement comparison page** available — transparent about accuracy

## Shopify Support
- **App store:** NO — not on Shopify App Store
- **Integration:** Enterprise/custom; targets retail chains, independent optometry, e-commerce platforms
- **Target segments:** Retail, Independent Optometry, E-commerce, Eyewear Brands

## Churn Signals
- No Shopify reviews (not in app store)

- Zendesk widget on site suggests customer support infrastructure

## Verdict
Topology sets the quality ceiling — precision measurements, photorealistic try-on, optometrist-grade accuracy. But they compete in a different league (enterprise B2B, not Shopify SMB). Their approach validates that measurement accuracy + try-on realism is the winning combination for premium eyewear. We should aim for their quality bar but at Shopify-accessible pricing.
