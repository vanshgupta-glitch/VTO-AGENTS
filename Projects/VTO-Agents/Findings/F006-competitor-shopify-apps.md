**Project:** [[VTO]] · Source task: [[T006 Competitor-Landscape-Map]]

## One-line takeaway
The Shopify App Store has 539 results for "virtual try on glasses eyewear" — but only a handful are eyewear-specific, and quality varies dramatically. FittingBox (4.7/5, 12 reviews) leads; most others are tiny (1-5 reviews) or general-purpose fashion VTO apps.

## Key Observations

### Market Structure
- **Tier 1 (Market Leader):** FittingBox — best rating + review count combo among eyewear-specific apps
- **Tier 2 (Established):** Auglio — more reviews than FB but lower rating (churn risk)
- **Tier 3 (New/Tiny):** TryOnMe, Virtual Try Glasses, Specfit — 1-2 reviews each, unproven
- **Tier 4 (Generalists):** Banuba, Camweara, GenLook, Antla — eyewear not core focus

### Churn & Quality Signals
- **Auglio's 3.5/5 is the big red flag** — with 16 reviews, this isn't just small sample noise. Something is wrong.
- **FittingBox's 4.7/5 with only 12 reviews** — moderate adoption, strong satisfaction. Room for a competitor.
- **Most apps have 0-5 reviews** — the Shopify eyewear VTO market is surprisingly underserved.
- **Camweara's 57 reviews at 4.9** is worth watching — general VTO but 57 reviews implies real traction.

### Pricing Landscape
- **Free tier** is the norm for entry-level (TryOnMe, Virtual Try Glasses, Specfit, Banuba, GenLook, Icona)
- **Free trial** for established players (FittingBox, Auglio)
- **$39/month** at the low end (Camweara)
- Our $19-149/mo range is competitive within this landscape

## Verdict
The Shopify eyewear VTO market is surprisingly WEAK. Only one clear leader (FittingBox), one struggling competitor (Auglio), and a handful of tiny apps. This is an open lane for a high-quality entrant. The opportunity is to enter at FittingBox's quality level but with (a) fully client-side processing, (b) better pricing, and (c) actual Shopify-native UX polish.
